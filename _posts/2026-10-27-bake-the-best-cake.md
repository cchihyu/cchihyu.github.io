You have 20 cakes to bake, and each recipe is a choice of sugar and oven temperature. Every cake you bake shows you how good that recipe tastes, so you have to decide when to explore new recipes and when to stay near the best one so far. This is the exploration–exploitation trade-off at the heart of bandit algorithms and Bayesian optimization.
