Imagine you are baking a cake and want it to taste as good as possible. You control two things: the amount of sugar (0–200 g) and the oven temperature (150–230 °C). You can bake 20 cakes. Click on the plot to choose a recipe, and the cake's taste (out of 5) is revealed after each click. Your goal is to find the recipe with the best taste: your score is the best taste among the cakes you bake.
