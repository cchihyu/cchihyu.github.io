A hidden function f lives on the interval [0, 1] (1-D games) or on the square [0, 1]2 (2-D games). You have a fixed budget of evaluations: 10 in a 1-D game and 20 in a 2-D game. At each step t you click on the plot to choose a point xt, and you see the reward f(xt). You do not know where the maximum is, or how high it is.

Your score is the cumulative regret over the T steps: the total reward you lost compared with choosing the best point x* every time,

RT = ∑t=1T ( f(x*) − f(xt) ).

Lower is better. Every evaluation counts, so you have to balance exploring new regions against exploiting the best region you have found so far. There are five 1-D games and five 2-D games, and everyone who picks the same game plays the same hidden function, so scores can be compared directly.
