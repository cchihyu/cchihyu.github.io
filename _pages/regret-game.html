A hidden function f lives on the interval [0, 1]. You have 10 evaluations. At each step t you click on the plot to choose a point xt, and you see the reward f(xt). You do not know where the maximum is, or how high it is.

Your score is the cumulative regret: the total reward you lost compared with choosing the best point x* every time,

R10 = ∑t=110 ( f(x*) − f(xt) ).

Lower is better. Every evaluation counts, so you have to balance exploring new regions against exploiting the best region you have found so far. For a class competition, everyone enters the same game number and so plays the same hidden function.
